# Privacy Policy Update - Data Collection & Usage

**Effective Date:** 2024-01-07

## 1. Information We Collect

During the admission and registration process, we collect the following personal and sensitive information:

*   **Personal Information:** Name, Date of Birth (DOB), Gender, Nationality.
*   **Contact Information:** Mobile Number, Email Address, Residential Address.
*   **Socio-Economic Data:** Caste Category (e.g., General, OBC, SC, ST), Annual Family Income.
*   **Health Data:** Blood Group, Allergies, Medical History, Medications.
*   **Financial Data:** Bank Account Number, IFSC Code (for Staff and Scholarship disbursements).

## 2. How We Use Your Data

The collected data is strictly used for academic and administrative purposes:

*   **Academic Records:** To maintain student registries, generate report cards, and issue transfer certificates.
*   **Communication:** To send alerts, attendance updates, and fee reminders to parents/guardians.
*   **Government Compliance:** Data regarding Caste and Income may be used for government reporting, scholarship eligibility, and reservation quota compliance as mandated by law.
*   **Health & Safety:** Medical history and blood group data are accessible only to authorized medical staff and school administration to handle emergencies.
*   **Financial Transactions:** Bank details are used solely for salary processing (Staff) or fee refunds/scholarships (Students).

## 3. Data Protection & Privacy

*   **Encryption:** Sensitive fields such as Bank Account Numbers and Medical History are encrypted at rest using AES-256 standards.
*   **Access Control:** Access to Personally Identifiable Information (PII) is restricted based on user roles. All access to sensitive data is logged for audit purposes.
*   **Right to be Forgotten:** Users have the right to request the deletion or anonymization of their personal data upon leaving the institution, subject to retention laws.
*   **Data Correction:** Parents can request corrections to their ward's data (e.g., Name, DOB) through the portal, subject to administrative approval.

## 4. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell or trade your personal data. Data may be shared with:
*   **Government Bodies:** Only when legally required.
*   **Payment Gateways:** For fee processing (strictly limited to transaction data).

For any privacy-related concerns, please contact our Data Protection Officer.
