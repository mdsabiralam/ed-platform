# Crisis Communication Templates

## 1. Service Outage Alert
**Subject:** [ALERT] Ed Platform Service Disruption - Investigation Underway
**To:** School Administrators, Principals

Dear Partner,

We are currently experiencing a service disruption affecting the **Ed Platform**. Our users may be unable to access the web portal and mobile app.

**Status:** Investigating
**Impact:** All services
**Next Update:** 30 Minutes

Our engineering team is actively diagnosing the issue. We have activated our Disaster Recovery protocols to restore services as quickly as possible.

We apologize for the inconvenience and appreciate your patience.

Sincerely,
The Ed Platform Team

## 2. Progress Update
**Subject:** [UPDATE] Ed Platform Service Status - Recovery in Progress
**To:** School Administrators, Principals

Dear Partner,

We have identified the root cause of the outage (Regional Infrastructure Failure). We are currently in the process of **failover to our backup region**.

**Estimated Resolution Time:** 45 Minutes
**Current Action:** Promoting backup systems and restoring connectivity.

Please note that you may see a "Maintenance Mode" screen intermittently.

Sincerely,
The Ed Platform Team

---

## 3. Service Restored
**Subject:** [RESOLVED] Ed Platform Service Restored
**To:** School Administrators, Principals

Dear Partner,

We are pleased to inform you that the **Ed Platform** is fully operational. You may now log in and use the application as normal.

**Summary:**
- **Issue:** Regional Data Center outage.
- **Resolution:** Failed over to Singapore (DR) region.
- **Data Integrity:** No data loss occurred (RPO < 5 mins).

Thank you for your understanding during this critical event.

Sincerely,
The Ed Platform Team
