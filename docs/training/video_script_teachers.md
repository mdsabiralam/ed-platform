# Video Script: Welcome to Ed-Platform (Teachers)

**Duration:** ~2 Minutes
**Target Audience:** Teachers (New Joiners)
**Tone:** Friendly, Professional, Reassuring.

---

**[0:00 - 0:15] Intro**
*   **Visual:** Host on screen (or animated character) with Ed-Platform Logo.
*   **Audio:** "Hello and welcome to Ed-Platform! We are thrilled to have you on board. Our goal is to simplify your daily administrative tasks so you can focus on what you do best—teaching. In the next 90 seconds, I'll show you the two most important features."

**[0:15 - 0:55] Feature 1: Voice Attendance**
*   **Visual:** Screen recording of the Mobile App. User taps "Attendance" -> Selects Class.
*   **Audio:** "First, let's look at Attendance. Gone are the days of manual registers. Open the app and tap 'Attendance'. Instead of tapping each name, simply click the Microphone icon..."
*   **Visual:** User clicks Mic, speaks "Mark roll number 5 and 12 absent". App highlights them.
*   **Audio:** "...and say, 'Mark Roll Number 5 and 12 absent'. Our AI instantly recognizes the command and updates the list. Click 'Save', and parents get an SMS instantly."

**[0:55 - 1:35] Feature 2: Homework Upload**
*   **Visual:** Screen recording of Web Dashboard. Teacher clicks "Homework" -> "Create New".
*   **Audio:** "Next, assigning homework. Click 'Create Homework'. You can type the details, but here's the magic—you can upload a photo of the whiteboard or a worksheet PDF."
*   **Visual:** Teacher uploads a PDF. Clicks "Assign".
*   **Audio:** "Click 'Assign', and it's sent to the entire class's mobile app. Students can view and submit their work right from their phones."

**[1:35 - 2:00] Outro**
*   **Visual:** Host returns. Support Portal URL (`support.edplatform.com`) on screen.
*   **Audio:** "It's that simple. If you ever get stuck, visit our Support Portal at support.edplatform.com or chat with our bot. Happy teaching!"
*   **Visual:** Fade to Black. Logo.
