# Service Level Agreement (SLA) - Premium Tier

**Effective Date:** [Date]
**Parties:** Ed-Platform (Service Provider) and [School Name] (Customer).

## 1. Uptime Guarantee
**Commitment:** Ed-Platform guarantees a Monthly Uptime Percentage of **99.9%** for the Core Services (Dashboard, API, Mobile App access).

**Calculation:**
`Monthly Uptime % = (Maximum Available Minutes - Downtime Minutes) / Maximum Available Minutes * 100`

**Service Credits:**
If we fail to meet the Uptime Guarantee, you are eligible for the following Service Credits:
- **< 99.9% - ≥ 99.0%:** 10% Credit on next monthly bill.
- **< 99.0% - ≥ 95.0%:** 25% Credit on next monthly bill.
- **< 95.0%:** 50% Credit on next monthly bill.

*Note:* Downtime excludes scheduled maintenance (notified 24h in advance) and force majeure events.

## 2. Data Privacy & Compliance
We are committed to protecting your data in accordance with:
- **DPDP Act (India):** We act as a Data Fiduciary for your staff data and a Data Processor for student data.
- **GDPR (EU):** For any EU residents, we comply with standard contractual clauses.

**Key Measures:**
- All data is encrypted at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.2+).
- Personally Identifiable Information (PII) is masked in logs and non-production environments.
- Regular security audits and vulnerability assessments.
