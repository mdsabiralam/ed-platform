# Video Script: Welcome to Ed-Platform (Teachers)

**Duration:** 2 Minutes
**Target Audience:** Teachers (New Users)

---

**[0:00 - 0:15] Intro**
*Visual: Friendly animated intro with the Ed-Platform logo. Soft, upbeat background music.*
**Narrator:** "Welcome to Ed-Platform! We are thrilled to have you on board. Our goal is to simplify your daily tasks so you can focus on what you do best—teaching. Let’s get you started."

**[0:15 - 0:50] Feature 1: Smart Attendance**
*Visual: Screen recording of the Mobile App Dashboard. Finger taps on the 'Attendance' icon.*
**Narrator:** "First, let's look at taking attendance. Forget the paper registers. Open the app and tap 'Attendance'."
*Visual: User selects 'Class 5A'. The list of students appears. User taps the Microphone icon.*
**Narrator:** "You can mark students present or absent manually, OR just use our Voice AI. Tap the mic and say, 'Mark Rahul and Priya as Absent'. The system updates instantly."
*Visual: Green ticks appear next to present students, red cross next to Rahul and Priya.*

**[0:50 - 1:30] Feature 2: Homework & Assignments**
*Visual: Transition to the 'Homework' tab on the Web Dashboard.*
**Narrator:** "Next, assigning homework. Click on the 'Homework' tab and select 'Create New'."
*Visual: User typing 'Science Project', selecting a due date, and clicking 'Attach File'.*
**Narrator:** "Enter the details, set a deadline, and here’s the best part—you can attach worksheets directly. Click 'Upload', select your file, and hit 'Send'. Parents get a notification immediately."
*Visual: Notification popping up on a parent’s phone: 'New Science Homework: Due Friday'.*

**[1:30 - 1:50] Outro & Support**
*Visual: Split screen showing Happy Teacher and Happy Student.*
**Narrator:** "That’s it! Fast attendance and easy homework management."
*Visual: Screen showing the 'Help' icon and the URL: support.ed-platform.com*
**Narrator:** "If you have any questions or run into trouble, just tap the 'Help' icon in the app or visit our Support Portal. We’re here for you."

**[1:50 - 2:00] End Card**
*Visual: Ed-Platform Logo. Text: "Empowering Education".*
**Narrator:** "Happy Teaching!"
*Fade out.*
